Questions about the screens, licensing, or an idea you would like to share? Choose the route that fits best.

## Report a bug or request a screen

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mantavyamstudios/wirefront/issues/new/choose) with the screen's name, the device you previewed it on and what you expected to happen. Requests for new screens are welcome there too.
